Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common payroll and HR queries below.

When is my salary credited every month?

Salaries are credited on the last working day of every month. If the last working day falls on a weekend or public holiday, the salary is credited on the preceding working day.

How can I download my payslip?

Go to Payroll Dashboard → Payslip, select the year and month, then click Download Slip. Payslips are available from the month you joined the organisation.

What is the PF deduction percentage?

Employee PF contribution is 12% of your Basic Wage. The employer contributes an equal 12%, split into 3.67% towards PF and 8.33% towards the Employee Pension Scheme (EPS).

Which tax regime is better for me – Old or New?

It depends on your investments and deductions. Use the Tax Calculator on this portal to compare both regimes side by side. Generally, if you have significant 80C investments, HRA, or home loan interest, the Old Regime may save more tax.

How do I submit a travel expense claim?

Go to Travel & Expenses and click New Claim. Attach receipts and provide a description. Claims are typically approved within 5 working days by your reporting manager.

What is Form 16 and when will I receive it?

Form 16 is a TDS certificate issued by your employer certifying the salary paid and tax deducted. It is generated annually by 15th June for the preceding financial year and can be downloaded from the Payroll Dashboard.

Can I change my tax regime mid-year?

For salaried employees, you can switch tax regimes only once at the beginning of a financial year (April) by submitting a declaration to the Payroll/HR team. Mid-year changes are generally not permitted under employer TDS provisions.

How do I raise a payroll grievance or query?

Go to Helpdesk → My Queries and click Raise Query. Select the query type, describe your issue, and submit. The payroll team will respond within 3 working days.
